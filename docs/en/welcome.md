Welcome to TrackLab, your tracking assistant. With it you can:

1. Track your activity with custom metrics and lists ([read more](./activities.md)).
2. Set up rules to build your routine and achieve your goals ([read more](./rules.md)).
3. Conduct experiments to understand what works personally for you ([read more](./experiments.md)).

Read [Getting Started](./getting-started.md) to make your first steps and understand basic mechanics.

If you have any questions, please check the documentation, or contact [tracklab@etsbox.com](mailto:tracklab@etsbox.com).
