This page walks you through your first few minutes with TrackLab.

## 1. Log an activity

You can start an activity three ways: 

- press "add" at the bottom of the history page
- press an action button on the [dashboard](./dashboard.md)
- follow [this link](/activity/add)

In the form, enter one or more actions (for example "Running"), set the date and time, and save. That is enough for a minimal activity.

## 2. Track more than time

For richer tracking, add two kinds of detail to an activity:

- [Metrics](./metrics.md) — number values, like Mood, Energy or Weight.
- [Library](./library.md) — your collection of lists, each holding items: food, places, people, purchases, projects, and so on.

Right in the activity form you can create a new metric or list on the spot, and it is saved for next time.

## 3. Look at the numbers

After creating several activities, you can see statistics. Statistics show how much time you spent on an action or list item, as well as how a metric's value changes over a period of time, with a calculated average.

## 4. Go further

Turn a habit into a commitment with [rules](./rules.md) — checks like "run at least 3 times a week" that show up on your dashboard. When you want to test whether a change actually helps, run an [experiment](./experiments.md): it ties metrics, lists and rules together to answer "did this change anything?".
