Stats turn your activities into graphs. Nothing appears until there is data — one activity is enough for the first point.

## What you can see

- How a metric moves over time — up to three at once, to compare them.
- How much time went to a specific action, tag or list item.

## Time reference

Metric graphs place each value at the activity's end time and interpolate between points, so the line reflects when things actually changed. Pick a date range at the top to focus any graph on a period.
