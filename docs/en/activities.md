An activity is the main tracking unit that has a date, start time, end time, actions, tags, custom lists and metrics.

## Time

An activity covers a fixed block of time from start to end. All of its actions are considered performed during that block. If you "worked, listened to music" from 12:00 to 15:00, you worked for three hours and listened to music for three hours.

One day lasts from 00:00 to 23:59. If an activity crosses midnight (for example starts at 23:50 and ends at 00:10), it counts as the last activity of the previous day.

## Adding an activity

Press "add" button on the bottom of the history page or an action button on the dashboard.
